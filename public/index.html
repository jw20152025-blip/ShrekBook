ShrekBook Policies

  1. Do not post inappropriate content.
  2. Respect other users and their opinions.
  3. Do not share personal information or sensitive data.
  4. Do not engage in harassment or bullying.
  5. Do not use ShrekBook for illegal activities.
  6. Do not create multiple accounts to manipulate the platform.
  7. Do not impersonate other users or public figures.
  8. Do not post spam or advertisements.
  9. Do not attempt to hack or exploit the platform.
  10. Do not share content that violates copyright or intellectual property rights.
  11. Do not post content that promotes violence or hate speech.
  12. Do not post misleading or false content.
  13. Do not post sexually explicit or pornographic content.
  14. Do not post discriminatory or offensive content.
  15. Do not message developers repeatedly about the same issue.
  16. Do not contact developers multiple times per day.
  17. Do not contact developers about unrelated issues.
  18. Do not contact developers about issues that are not your own.
  19. Do not contact developers about non-urgent matters.
  20. If you suspect a violation of these policies, contact the ShrekBook support team.
